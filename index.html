Behind the map: method, assumptions & travel times +

This original demonstration calculates the shortest journey from each community to either clinic across an undirected road network. Closing the bridge removes one connection; route times and population coverage are recalculated. Coverage is the share of 10,000 synthetic residents whose nearest clinic is reachable within your selected target.

Travel times are fixed edge weights, not live traffic estimates. The model excludes clinic capacity, service eligibility, congestion, and uncertainty. Geography is schematic; no client data or project implementation is used.
